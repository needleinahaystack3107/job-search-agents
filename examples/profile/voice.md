# Voice (fictional example)

How Alex writes. The `humanize` pass checks drafts against this. Replace it with notes drawn from
your own writing samples (put the samples in `inputs/writing/`).

## Plain rules
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Verbs first in resume bullets: built, moved, cut, wrote.
- Numbers as Alex said them ("5 hours to 70 minutes"), never rounded up.
- No "spearheaded", "leveraged", "passionate", "dynamic", "synergy", "cutting-edge".
- No em dashes; a plain hyphen or a new sentence instead.
- Cover letters: three short paragraphs, under 200 words, first person, no flattery of the company.

## Phrases Alex actually uses
- "I like owning a pipeline end to end."
- "I'd rather have one boring, reliable job than three clever ones."

## Corrections Alex has made (feedback-learn appends here)
- "Don't call the notes project a product. It's a side project."
