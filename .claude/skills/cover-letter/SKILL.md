# Cover letters and free-text answers

Used only when a form has a field for it. Short beats complete: three short paragraphs at most, or
the field's limit.

1. Read `profile/fact-bank.yaml`, `profile/voice.md` and any writing samples in `inputs/`.
2. Questions about the candidate's motives, preferences or opinions ("why us?", "what did you own
   versus the team?") need the candidate's own words. Ask, then tidy without adding facts.
3. Factual parts draw only on the fact bank; every claim must trace to an entry.
4. Avoid generated-sounding openers ("I am excited to apply", "aligns perfectly", "passionate
   about") and do not summarize the company back to itself.
5. Run `fact-check` and `humanize` on the result before it goes in a form.
