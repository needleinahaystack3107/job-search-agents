# Recruiter read

A recruiter gives a resume a few seconds: the top third, titles, employers and numbers. This pass
checks that what they catch is what the posting asks for.

1. Read only the posting's title and its first five requirements, then the resume. Do not read
   `match.md` or the fact bank first; simulate someone who has neither.
2. Skim: name line, current title and employer, the first two bullets, bold text, numbers, skills.
3. From the skim alone, write down the three things you remember; which of the first five
   requirements you saw evidence for; what would make you hesitate (level, location, visa, a
   missing must-have, a claim that sounds too large); and which interview question each large
   claim invites.
4. Read the whole resume once and note anything strong buried below the skim line.
5. Write the `Recruiter read` section of `review.md` with a score out of 10 and specific moves:
   which bullet to raise or cut, which number to bring forward.

Large impact figures draw scrutiny. A claim passes if the wording says who estimated it and what
the candidate's own part was. Suggested moves are reordering, cutting and emphasis only.
